Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Golf Majors Pool is a season-long golf pool for the four men's majors, played in private leagues with friends. It is operated by Tucker App Studio LLC in Abilene, Texas. This policy covers the iPhone app and the website at golfmajorspool.com.

Your account

To play, you make an account. It holds:

  • your display name, which the other members of your leagues see on the standings;
  • your email address, used to sign you in and to send a password reset link when you ask for one;
  • your password, stored only as a salted hash, never as the password itself;
  • the sign-in sessions on your devices (each ends after 30 days unused), and which league you last had open.

Your leagues and picks

For each league you belong to, we store your membership and role (member or commissioner), your roster of golfers and tie-break answer for each tournament, and when you submitted them. A league's name, join code and payout settings are stored with the league. Your picks for a tournament are hidden from the other members until that tournament starts; after that, everyone in the league can see them, because comparing rosters is the game.

Purchases

League season passes are bought in the iPhone app through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat, which manages in-app purchases for us, tells our server which passes belong to your account, identified by your account's internal ID rather than your name or email. We keep a record of each purchase (what was bought, when, and which league and season it was used on) so a pass is applied once and your league stays unlocked for the season.

What stays on your device

  • Your sign-in: a random token kept by the app (a cookie on the website). Your password is never stored on the device. The token renews itself while you use the app, so you stay signed in, and it ends after 30 days unused or when you sign out.
  • Recent tournament data, so the leaderboard opens quickly.

What we don't collect

The app does not ask for or collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Golf data

Leaderboards, player statistics, rankings and fields come from public sources such as ESPN and the PGA Tour. That information is about professional golfers, not about you.

Services we use

  • Vercel hosts the app and website. Like any web server, it keeps standard request logs, which include IP addresses, for a short time.
  • Supabase stores the accounts, leagues, picks and purchase records described above, and a hosted Redis store keeps tournament data and settings.
  • Resend sends password reset emails.
  • Apple processes in-app purchases, and RevenueCat records them for us, as described under Purchases.
  • Capgo delivers app updates. It receives the app's version and an anonymous device identifier so it can send the right update.

Children

Golf Majors Pool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app or on the website: open your league (the league name at the top of the standings) and choose Delete my account at the bottom of the Leagues panel, then type DELETE to confirm. That permanently removes your account, its sign-in sessions, your picks and tie-breaks in every league, and your purchase records. A league in which you are the only member, and its commissioner, is deleted with it. If you are the only commissioner of a league that has other members, you'll be asked to make another member commissioner first, so the league isn't left without one.

You can also ask us to delete your account or any of your information by emailing the address below.
