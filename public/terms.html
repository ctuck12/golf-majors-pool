What Golf Majors Pool is

Golf Majors Pool lets private groups of friends run a pool on The Players Championship and the four men's majors. Each league has a commissioner who invites members, sets the league's entry fee and payouts, and runs the league. Members pick golfers under a salary cap, and standings are scored automatically from live tournament results.
